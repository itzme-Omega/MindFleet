Help & how to play

Rules, scoring and a quick walkthrough of a MindFleet match.

The rules of the arena

  1. Two fleets, one grid. Each player hides five ships on a 10 × 10 grid: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2).
  2. Deploy your fleet. Before the match begins, arrange your ships. Pick a ship, tap a square to move it, and use Rotate to change direction. Once the match starts, the layout is locked.
  3. Attack by tapping a cell. Choosing a cell on the opponent's grid opens a question. The strike is only resolved after the question.
  4. Be first to answer correctly. If you answer first and correctly, your shot lands. If your opponent answers first, they score and may reposition one of their ships. If nobody solves it in time, the cell is marked and the turn passes.
  5. Hit again. A successful strike gives you another turn. Sink every enemy ship to win.
  6. Bonus rounds. After every third successful strike a bonus question appears for extra points.
  7. Timers. Physics and Mathematics questions allow 3 minutes. Chemistry questions allow 1–3 minutes depending on question type.

🎯 Scoring

A correct answer earns 10 points. A ship hit earns 10 more. Your XP bar (with your level) sits at the top of the screen and your current rating is on the dashboard. A win adds 15–25 rating points and a loss removes the same amount.

📚 Subjects & chapters

Use Configure Preset on the dashboard to choose your quiz mode, subjects and chapters. Questions in a battle come from the chapters in your preset.

🤖 Bots or people

Pick Play with Bots to start instantly against a demo opponent. Use Multiplayer to create a private room, join a friend's code, or enter the global queue.

📈 Rank & history

Win matches to climb the leaderboard. Your finished matches are listed under Settings → History.

🔊 Sound

Use the music icon in the top bar to change the volume or mute the game sounds.

👤 Profile

Click your name at the top to see matches played, win rate and to log out.
